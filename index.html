About Chitragupta (AICTE-Verify)

Smart India Hackathon 2026 • Problem Statement 26261 • Regulation Bureau

The Problem

Every year, the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) scrutinizes tens of thousands of Extension of Approval (EoA) and new institutional approval applications. Scrutiny officers manually examine multi-gigabyte document bundles comprising land deeds, architectural plans, fire NOCs, audited accounts, and faculty rosters. This manual bottleneck creates inspection backlogs, risks overlooking forged or expired certificates, and lacks unified cross-document reconciliation.

Our Solution

Chitragupta (AICTE-Verify) is an intelligent, privacy-preserving, decision-support module seamlessly embedded within the AICTE Partner Portal. Combining localized OCR, deterministic regulatory rule packs, multi-channel sandbox connectors, forensic image tampering detectors, and an agentic RAG explainer with strict human gates, Chitragupta compresses 45 minutes of manual scrutiny into 2 minutes while maintaining 100% human accountability.

Why "Chitragupta"?

In Indian tradition, Chitragupta is the meticulous divine record-keeper who maintains the Agrasamdhani—the complete, unerring ledger of all actions. Our platform embodies this spirit: an incorruptible, vigilant record-keeper that observes every certificate, verifies every clause, and commits every decision into an immutable cryptographic chain.

Non-Negotiable Regulatory Safeguards

  • Decision Support Only: AI outputs are strictly preliminary indicators. Final approval rests exclusively with authorized human committees.
  • No Autonomous Adverse Decisions: Rejections or intake reductions require digitally signed human authorization.
  • Sovereign On-Premise Execution: No institutional records or scanned documents leave Indian sovereign server boundaries.
